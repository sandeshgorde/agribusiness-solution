# Risk Register

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stale market data | Medium | High | Source/timestamp, expiry, graceful fallback |
| Low digital literacy | High | High | Simple mobile-first UX, multilingual readiness |
| Privacy/security breach | Medium | High | Least privilege, secure auth, audit logs |
| Scope creep | High | Medium | Freeze P0; defer AI/payments |
| External API outage | Medium | Medium | Adapter layer, timeouts, cached last-known data |
| Inventory inconsistency | Medium | High | Transactions and concurrency controls |
| Fake users/fraud | Medium | High | Verification, reporting and audit trail |
| Logistics mismatch | Medium | Medium | Validate capacity and delivery details |
